# Loyalty and Conquest Mechanics

Source: Travian Support — [Loyalty](https://support.travian.com/en/support/solutions/articles/7000060248-loyalty), [Conquering Villages](https://support.travian.com/en/support/solutions/articles/7000064014-conquering-villages), [Preventing Conquering](https://support.travian.com/en/support/solutions/articles/7000060247-preventing-conquering), [Town Hall / Master Builder / Celebrations](https://support.travian.com/en/support/solutions/articles/7000062813-master-builder).

## Loyalty Basics

- Every village has loyalty from 0% to 125% (not 0–100).
- Starting value: **100%**.
- Recovery: **+1%/hour**, up to 100% by default, or **125%** with a Great Celebration running.
- Loyalty 0 → village changes owner at the moment the chief-bearing wave lands and wins.

## Loyalty-Dropping Units

These units reduce loyalty when they attack successfully as part of a winning wave. Each wave drops loyalty by a random **20–30%**.

Residence and Palace are **mutually exclusive** in the same village.

## Chief Wave Arithmetic

Defender at full loyalty (100%) with no celebration:

- Best case per wave (30% drop): **4 waves** to zero
- Average (25% drop): **5 waves**
- Worst case (20% drop): **5–6 waves**

Defender at 125% loyalty (Great Celebration active):

- Typically need one additional wave

Defender with continuous Great Celebrations + Tablet of Law:

- Can maintain 100%+ loyalty indefinitely unless attacker out-paces celebrations

## Great Celebration (defense-grade anti-conquest)

- **Small Celebration**: 6,400 wood / 6,650 clay / 4,000 iron / 9,000 crop → 24 h, +500 CP
- **Great Celebration**: 16,000 / 17,000 / 12,000 / 28,000 → 60 h, +2,000 CP, requires Town Hall Lv 10
- **Tablet of Law** hero item: instant +5% loyalty

Costs scale with server speed.

## Solo Chiefing Setup

Hardcore players run two 3-chief villages:

- Hammer village (non-capital): Palace Lv 20 with 3 chief slots
- Support village (non-capital): Residence Lv 20 with 3 chief slots

= 6 chiefs per combined wave → possible to take undefended enemies without alliance coordination.

## Preventing Conquest

- Keep loyalty ≥ 101% at all times (cap at 125% via celebrations)
- Town Hall Lv 10 + Great Celebration chain
- Residence Lv 10+ in target village (enemy chiefs fight your chiefs)
- Alliance defense timed between enemy waves — any single wave failing prevents that wave's loyalty drop
