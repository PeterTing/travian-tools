# Hero System

Source: Travian Support hero articles — [Hero overview](https://support.travian.com/en/support/solutions/articles/7000061665-hero-overview), [Adventures](https://support.travian.com/en/support/solutions/articles/7000060172-adventures), [Hero Health and Revival](https://support.travian.com/en/support/solutions/articles/7000064020-hero-health-and-revival), [Hero Experience](https://support.travian.com/en/support/solutions/articles/7000060173-hero-experience), [Hero items (all slots)](https://support.travian.com/en/support/solutions/articles/7000064021-hero-item-overview-and-mounts), [Auctions](https://support.travian.com/en/support/solutions/articles/7000065116-auctions).

## Hero Basics

- One hero per account. Revivable if killed.
- Typically reaches Level 100. Each level grants **4 attribute points** to distribute.
- Four attributes:
  1. **Power** — hero HP regen per day (base 100 HP)
  2. **Off Bonus** — % bonus to troops attacking *with* the hero
  3. **Def Bonus** — % bonus to troops defending *where* the hero is stationed
  4. **Resources** — hero produces resources in home village

## Adventures

- Triggered from Hero's Mansion. New adventure spawns on a timer (short ≈ 4 h, long ≈ 24 h).
- Travel time: 1–2 h depending on distance.
- Difficulty affects risk and reward:
  - Short adventures: low HP cost, modest rewards
  - Long adventures: higher HP risk, better loot (items, troops, resources, silver, XP)

**Early-game rule (Lumi guide):** the **first adventure** ALWAYS rewards a horse (mount) — run a short adventure immediately.

## Hero Items

### Slots

Helmet / Body Armor / Boots / Right Hand / Left Hand / Horse / Bag (consumables).

### Tiers

- Tier 1 (common, gray)
- Tier 2 (rare, blue)
- Tier 3 (unique, purple)

### Right-Hand (tribe-specific weapon, tier-3 examples)

Roman Imperial Regalia; Gaul Haeduan weapon; Teuton Teutonic Knight weapon; similar tribe-specific unique weapons for Egyptian / Hun / Spartan / Viking.

### Left Hand

Maps (reveal coordinates), shields (def bonus), flags (tribe-wide bonus), scrolls (culture points).

### Boots

Cavalry speed boots (+15 to +30%), infantry speed boots.

### Armor

Hero HP, XP gain rate, resource production.

### Helmets

Hero XP, cavalry/infantry bonus, anti-catapult.

### Consumables (Bag)

- **Ointment** — instant heal
- **Bucket** — +10 HP
- **Bandage / Great Bandage** — revive fallen troops after battle
- **Cage** — capture wild animals from oases for defense
- **Tablet of Law** — +5% loyalty to home village
- **Artwork** — +2,000 culture points
- **Book of Wisdom** — reset hero attribute allocation
- **Scroll** — +10 XP
- **Tribute** — gold farming (limited use)

## Auctions

- Items trade via Auction House using **silver**.
- Silver sources: adventures, selling unwanted items, gold → silver (1 gold = 100 silver).
- Auctions typically run 24 h.

## Health and Revival

- HP regen: +1 to +25 HP/day depending on Power attribute and Armor.
- Combat damage proportional to enemy strength relative to hero strength.
- When HP reaches 0 the hero dies. Revival cost scales with hero level:
  - Low level: small crop cost + short wait
  - High level: substantial crop cost or gold shortcut
- While dead: no off/def bonus active, no adventures possible.

## Oasis Claiming

- **Hero's Mansion** (GID 37) Lv 10 / 15 / 20 unlocks 1 / 2 / 3 oasis claims.
- Claim process: hero attacks oasis until all wild animals are cleared.
- Claimed oasis boosts the claiming village's resource production.
- Global cap on claimed oases per player (server-dependent, usually 3).

## Strategy — Early Game

From Lumi guide:

1. First adventure → horse (don't waste it)
2. Prioritize **Power** attribute early → survive adventures
3. Use hero to farm oases (adventures + direct attacks) for resources
4. After ~6th adventure, switch allocation toward **Resources** (or specific off/def bonus based on account role)
5. Hero's Mansion in feeders: only if oasis available. Demolish after claiming so adventures don't spawn near feeders.
